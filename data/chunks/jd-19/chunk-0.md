## Data Engineer (Senior)
**Department:** Data
**Seniority Level:** Senior
**Job Family:** Data Platform
### Summary
We are seeking a highly motivated and experienced Senior Data Engineer to join our growing Data team. As a Senior Data Engineer, you will be a key contributor to building and maintaining our data platform, ensuring its reliability, scalability, and security. You will design, develop, and implement data pipelines, optimize data storage and retrieval, and collaborate with data scientists and analysts to enable data-driven decision-making across the organization. You will play a critical role in maintaining our existing pipelines on Airflow + Spark (AWS EMR) and contribute to future platform enhancements.
